The weekly bar
First number: how much you used. Second: how much of the week is gone.
███░░░░░░░30% / 60% Week
Under Pace

You are using less than the week so far. Relax, keep going.

█████░░░░░52% / 55% Week
On Pace

Right on track to last the whole week.

██████░░░░61% / 55% Week
Ahead Of Pace

A bit fast. Use lighter models or shorter chats for a while.

████████░░80% / 55% Week
Over Pace

At this speed you run out before the week resets. Slow down.

Example: 58% / 64% means you used 58% of your weekly limit, and 64% of the week has passed. You are fine.
